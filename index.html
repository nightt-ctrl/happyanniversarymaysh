For You

I don't really know if you'll ever see this. Honestly, I doubt you will. But somehow, I still wanted to write it.

I miss you.

I miss the little things more than anything. Your smile, your laugh, the way you made ordinary moments feel special. I miss talking to you about random things, spending time together, and just knowing that you were there. You still are, but just not in the way it was you know?

I still think about all the memories we made. The moments that probably seemed so small at the time, but mean so much to me now. The smiles, the hugs, the laughs, the places we went, the little gifts we gave each other, the sneaky make outs, and all those moments where I felt genuinely happy just because I was with you.

Sometimes I'll come across something that reminds me of you, and for a moment, it feels like everything comes back.

And I still love you so much my pretty girl.

I don't say that expecting anything from you. I don't want this to make you feel guilty, or like you owe me something. I just don't want to lie about how I feel.

You meant so much to me.

You were someone I could laugh with, be myself around, and make memories with. And even though things didn't end the way I wished they would, that doesn't erase all the good that happened between us.

But I'm also grateful.

Grateful that I got to know you. Grateful for every smile you gave me. Grateful for every hug, every conversation, every silly moment, every gift, every memory, and every second that made me feel lucky to have you.

If I could go back, I wouldn't erase any of it.

Even the memories that hurt now are still memories I wouldn't trade.

I don't know what the future holds, and maybe one day this feeling will become something quieter. Maybe I'll look back at all of this and smile instead of feeling that ache in my chest. But I probably won't. Lmaoo. It'll just ache because of how much I love ya.

But right now, I just miss you.

And a part of me will probably always care about you. Well, all of me.

Thank you for being such a beautiful part of my life.

I hope you're happy.

And wherever life takes you, I hope you always remember that there was someone who loved you deeply, cherished every memory with you, and will always be grateful that you were once a part of his story.

I miss you, sayang.

I still love you. ❤️ My little princess. Happy anniversary.

— From someone who still cares.
